# Built-in image-generation prompts

## Pass 1 — complete surprise and fist-shake sheet

Use case: identity-preserve  
Asset type: production source sprite sheet for a Godot 2D game character  
Input images: Image 1 is the exact current in-game diner identity and pose/style
reference; Image 2 is the exact current in-game walk/run identity, proportions,
scale, and pixel-rendering reference.  
Primary request: Create one new 6-column by 2-row sprite animation source sheet
containing exactly 12 complete full-body frames of this same adult diner, all
facing screen-right. Top row, frames 1-6 in chronological order: a short
theft-surprise beat. She first looks down at the now-empty serving hand, visibly
realizes the food is gone, recoils briefly without moving her planted foot
baseline, then lifts her gaze and turns attention toward the escaping gull to
screen-right. Bottom row, frames 1-6 in chronological order: a harmless
failed-chase anger beat. She has stopped and planted both feet, faces
screen-right, keeps the former food hand empty and safely lowered/open, and
shakes the other free clenched fist angrily twice. Alternate the raised fist
between two nearby positions to read as two shakes while the feet remain
planted.  
Style/medium: match the reference pixel-art rendering exactly: chunky clean
pixels, dark navy outlines, warm brown skin, navy textured hair under coral-red
visor, turquoise hoop earring, teal-and-white striped polo, navy high-waisted
shorts with tiny yellow belt buckle, turquoise-and-white sandals.  
Composition/framing: transparent canvas; 6 equal columns and 2 equal rows; one
centered complete character per cell; generous transparent gutters; identical
scale and body volume in every frame; identical shared foot baseline; no
cropping, overlap, grid lines, labels, or separators.  
Constraints: preserve this exact adult identity, face, hair, visor, earring,
clothes, footwear, proportions, adult scale, outline weight, palette, and
right-facing orientation. Both hands are empty in all 12 frames. No food
anywhere. Keep both feet aligned and planted throughout the entire bottom row.
The fist shake is expressive but non-contact: no strike, swat, lunge, attack
arc, impact effect, weapon, hitbox cue, or contact pose. No body-size changes.
No extra characters or scenery. No text, logo, watermark, shadows, or glow.

## Pass 2 — arm-role correction

Change only the six bottom-row frames. In every bottom-row frame, keep the
character facing screen-right and swap the arm roles: the screen-right/front
former serving hand must stay empty, open, and safely lowered near the hip; the
screen-left/rear arm must be bent upward and its clenched fist must perform the
angry two-shake animation. Alternate that rear fist between two nearby raised
positions across the six frames while both feet remain completely planted.
Keep the entire top row unchanged and preserve the exact transparent 6x2 layout,
identity, proportions, scale, baseline, clothing, palette and pixel rendering.
Both hands remain empty; no food, strike, swat, lunge, attack arc, contact pose,
weapon, text, watermark, shadow, grid, or labels.

## Pass 3 — viewer-side correction

Edit only the six characters in the bottom row. Move each raised clenched fist
to the viewer's left side of the character, the same side as the turquoise hoop
earring. Move the arm currently on the viewer's right down beside the shorts and
make that hand open and empty. The viewer-left, earring-side fist alternates near
shoulder and above head while both feet stay planted. Keep the top row visually
unchanged and preserve the exact transparent layout, identity, adult scale,
baseline, outfit, palette and pixel rendering. No food, contact action, weapon,
extra element, text, watermark, shadow, grid, or label.

## Final fist-shake strip

Use case: identity-preserve  
Asset type: six-frame production source animation strip for a Godot 2D game
character  
Input images: Images 1 and 2 are the exact current in-game diner identity,
scale, palette, and pixel-art references. Image 3 shows the desired raised-fist
concept.  
Primary request: Create exactly one horizontal row of six complete full-body
frames of this same adult diner facing screen-right, showing a harmless planted
angry fist shake after a failed chase. Across frames 1-6, move the same empty
fist between shoulder height and above-head height, reading as two firm shakes.
The other empty hand hangs safely downward beside the shorts below waist height.
Both feet stay planted in exactly the same positions.  
Style/medium: exact reference pixel art, chunky clean pixels, dark navy outlines,
warm brown skin, navy textured hair under coral-red visor, turquoise hoop
earring, teal-and-white striped polo, navy high-waisted shorts with yellow

one centered complete character per cell; identical adult scale, body volume,

labels, or grid.  
Constraints: preserve exact identity, face, outfit, proportions, outline weight,
palette, and screen-right facing direction. Both hands empty. No food. No swat,
