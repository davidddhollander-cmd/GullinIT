# Three gull species — concept review, 19 October 2026

Delivered nine alternative designs: three per species, each with standing and flying views. Full generated artwork, exact built-in image-generation prompts, source links and saved reference photos are in `assets/reference/gull-species-v1/`. Source research comes from RSPB, WWT and BTO; see that folder's `SOURCES.md` for specific pages and photo attribution. Adult breeding plumage keeps the species readable without juvenile pattern variations.

| Species | Game role | A | B | C |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Herring gull | Standard | Coastal | Chip Thief | Harbour Rogue |
| Black-legged kittiwake | Smaller and faster | Coastal | Quick Flick | Sea Dart |
| Great black-backed gull | Larger | Coastal | Big Boss | Breakwater Bruiser |

The user approved **Herring B / Chip Thief**, **Kittiwake C / Sea Dart**, **Great Black-backed C / Breakwater Bruiser**: a friendly full-bodied standard gull, a more angular and energetic small gull, and the broadest heavyweight silhouette. A options retain more neutral bird proportions; B options round the head/chest; C options emphasize posture and angularity. A and B differences are deliberately modest compared with the C silhouettes. These are alternatives to choose between, not new animation frames. See `GULL_SPECIES_PRODUCTION_V1.md` for their production implementation.

The three full-detail boards are `assets/previews/gull-species-herring-v1.png`, `gull-species-kittiwake-v1.png` and `gull-species-great-black-backed-v1.png`. A fourth board, `gull-species-size-comparison-v1.png`, compares B at proposed standing heights of 22, 17 and 28 logical pixels. Species boards show enlarged detail; the size board shows a shared game scale. All three use the approved diner's outline and cel/pixel shading as the rendering reference.

Visual review checked adult plumage: pale grey/pink-legged herring gull with a red lower-bill spot, black-legged kittiwake with an unmarked bill and uninterrupted black wing ends, and a heavy near-black-backed adult with pink legs. A herring flight tail was corrected to white. Nine unique source sheets and all 18 compact transparent preparations were verified; full-detail cutouts were also rendered and visually inspected. The isolated compact previews have binary alpha. No gameplay suite was required for this artwork-only concept pass.

The current production gull registry and movement tuning are outside this concept deliverable. The kittiwake's extra speed is a requested gameplay trait; no biological speed claim or numerical balance commitment is implied. Each chosen species can be assigned to player or rival roles independently. The next production pass should build complete movement/contact/landing/perch animation coverage, then align beaks, feet, game size and species tuning together. Preserve natural species colours and use role/name cues rather than applying one global rival tint across all three.
